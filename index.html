Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how PocketAI Server handles information when you use the app and its related services.

Effective date: 15 November 2026 Applies to PocketAI Server for Android

1. About this policy

PocketAI Server (“PocketAI”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is an Android application that lets you download compatible AI models, run them on your device, and optionally make your device's model available to compatible applications through our hosting service.

This policy describes information that may be handled by the PocketAI Android app and the hosting gateway. It also explains the choices available to you.

2. How PocketAI processes your AI requests

Using a model on your phone

When you use the app's on-device AI features, the selected model performs inference on your Android device. The model processes the text you submit locally. Downloading a model requires an internet connection, but using an already downloaded model for local inference does not inherently require one.

When you turn on hosting

If you enable hosting so another application can use your phone's model, requests are routed through the PocketAI gateway to your device. Your phone performs the model inference; the gateway helps connect the requesting application to your phone and relay the response.

Hosted requests are therefore not a direct, phone-only connection. Request content is transmitted over the network and handled by the gateway as part of routing. Do not submit information through hosted requests unless you are comfortable with this processing.

Third-party applications

When you connect another application to PocketAI Server, that application may send prompts, conversation context, and other request parameters to the hosting service. The third-party application's own privacy policy also applies to information it collects or processes.

3. Information that may be processed

Depending on the features you use, the following information may be processed while the app and services operate:

  • AI request content: prompts, messages, and request parameters sent to the model, including through hosted integrations. Hosted request content is relayed through the gateway to your device.
  • Model information: model identifiers, download status, model version, and model-related operational details handled by the app.
  • Authentication information: API credentials required to authenticate access to the hosting service.
  • Connection information: technical information needed to establish and maintain the hosting connection and route requests.

4. API keys and credentials

PocketAI may provide an API key or other credential for connecting compatible applications to your hosted model. Treat these credentials like passwords: do not share them publicly, include them in public repositories, or expose them in client-side code you do not control.

The app is designed to store sensitive credentials using Android secure storage facilities. The backend stores the API credentials needed for authentication in a form that is not readable to the service operator. Revoke or rotate a credential if you believe it has been exposed. A credential grants access according to the permissions and controls implemented by the service.

5. How information is used

Information processed by PocketAI and its supporting services may be used to:

  • Download, verify, load, and manage AI model files.
  • Authenticate and maintain a hosting connection between your device and the gateway.
  • Route hosted requests to your device and return model responses to the requesting application.
  • Display request activity and service status in the app.
  • Maintain the reliability and security of the service and prevent misuse.

6. Storage and retention

Downloaded model files are stored on your device until you delete them or remove the app, subject to Android's storage behavior. App settings and credentials may also be stored locally to support the features you use.

The PocketAI backend stores only the API credentials required to authenticate access to the hosting service. These credentials are stored in a form that is not readable to the service operator.

Other than these API credentials, PocketAI does not store your prompts, model responses, conversation history, downloaded model files, or other user content on its backend servers. Hosted requests are relayed through the gateway to your device for inference, and the response is returned to the requesting application.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. The PocketAI backend stores only the API credentials needed to authenticate access to the hosting service. Those credentials are not readable to the service operator. PocketAI does not store prompts, model responses, conversation history, or other user content on its backend servers.

Information may be disclosed where required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect users or the service.

8. Your choices and controls

  • You can use local inference without enabling hosting.
  • You can choose whether to enable or stop hosting from within the app.
  • You can delete downloaded models using the app's model controls.
  • You can avoid sending sensitive or confidential information in prompts, especially when using hosted integrations.
  • You can revoke or rotate exposed API credentials where the service provides that control.
  • You can uninstall the app to stop its use on your device.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled by PocketAI. However, no application, network transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Keep your device updated, protect access to your phone, and keep API keys and other credentials private. If you suspect unauthorized access to your hosted service, stop hosting and rotate or revoke affected credentials where possible.

10. Children's privacy

PocketAI is not designed specifically for children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us using the details below so the concern can be reviewed.

11. International processing

If you use hosting or other online features, information may be transmitted over the internet and routed through infrastructure that may be located outside your country. The model inference for hosted requests takes place on your Android device.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as PocketAI's features or data practices change. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will take reasonable steps to bring it to your attention.
